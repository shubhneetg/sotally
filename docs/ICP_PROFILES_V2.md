# Sotally — ICP Profiles V2 (Creator Platform Vision)

## Overview

Sotally is a platform where ANYONE can create software apps by describing what they want in natural language. The AI generates the app, deploys it to their storefront (name.sotally.com), and they get followers, community, and revenue. No coding. No technical skill. Just describe and it exists.

This document maps **12 primary ICPs** across 10 micro-niches, each with **4 downstream users** who ALSO become creators, producing **48 end-user personas** and approximately **~250 tools** across the entire ecosystem.

### The Two-Layer Creator Effect

In Sotally's model, the line between "creator" and "user" dissolves:
- A wellness coach creates a mood tracker
- Her user (a yoga studio owner) uses it, then creates her OWN yoga pose lookup
- The yoga studio's student uses THAT, then creates a meditation timer
- Every user is a potential creator. Every creator was first a user.
