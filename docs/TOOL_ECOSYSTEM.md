## Key Patterns & Insights

### 1. Education is the killer category
Emma Zhang's 20 academic tools generate $1.1M/month — more than the next two creators combined. The driver: massive TAM (20M+ US college students), viral distribution (students share tools that save their grades), low per-use pricing, and the most emotionally charged outcome metrics (GPA impact). **AnkiForge alone at $200K/month is a venture-scale product.**

### 2. Volume beats price
Tom Nguyen's highest-revenue tool (LinkedIn Comment Strategy Engine, $25K/month) costs just 1 credit ($0.50) per use. But LinkedIn creators use it 200 times/month. Low friction + high frequency = highest individual revenue. The lesson: design for daily habits, not occasional use.

### 3. Workflow suites create lock-in
Every downstream customer has 5 tools that form an interconnected workflow. David Okafor's local agency tools (Content Factory + GBP Optimizer + Campaign Planner + Onboarding + Retention Dashboard) form a complete agency operating system. Using one makes the next more valuable. Sotally's bundling and subscription features are critical for creators.

### 4. Domain specificity is the moat
A "cold email writer" is a commodity. A "Prospect Intelligence Brief that feeds a personalized cold email sequence calibrated to prospect seniority" is a must-have. Priya Sharma knows a dentist's review response cannot reference specific procedures (HIPAA). This embedded domain knowledge is what generic AI cannot replicate.

### 5. SaaS replacement is the stickiest play
Carlos Mendez's tools have the highest per-customer revenue ($200-800 credits/month) with the smallest TAM. But once you replace Zendesk AI ($1,300/month) with a $300/month Sotally tool, you do not switch back. David Kim's entire support stack (5 tools) replaces $2,500/month in Zendesk + Intercom.

### 6. "Career insurance" tools have zero price sensitivity
Rachel's Pipeline Attribution Tagger protects her $600K budget. Alex's SEO Optimizer protects his job. These tools prevent catastrophic outcomes. Price sensitivity drops to near-zero when the alternative is career damage.

### 7. Non-creator buyers generate massive demand
Rachel Kim and Mike Torres do not create tools, but the tools they BUY (built by niche creators) represent $675K/month in combined creator revenue. This proves that even non-creator personas create massive pull on the marketplace.

### 8. Prompt engineers are Sotally's killer creator category
Tom Nguyen's outsized revenue ($109K/month) from near-zero marginal cost tools with massive addressable markets (every LinkedIn user, every email marketer) signals that Sotally should actively recruit prompt engineers. Their tools have the best unit economics on the platform.
